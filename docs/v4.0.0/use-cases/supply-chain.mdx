Aqua Protocol provides cryptographic provenance tracking for supply chain operations, enabling end-to-end traceability from raw materials to finished products. Each step in the supply chain is recorded as a signed revision, creating a verifiable chain of custody that proves authenticity and tracks movement across multiple parties.

## Core capabilities

### End-to-end traceability
Track products from origin to destination with cryptographic proof at each step. Every transfer, transformation, and quality check is recorded in a tamper-evident Aqua tree, creating a complete product history that can be independently verified.

### Multi-party coordination
Supply chains involve manufacturers, distributors, retailers, regulators, and consumers. Aqua Protocol enables all parties to contribute to the tree while maintaining data integrity, without requiring a central authority.

### Provenance verification
Prove the origin and authenticity of products cryptographically. Consumers and businesses can verify that products are genuine, sourced ethically, and meet quality standards without trusting intermediaries.

### Real-time transparency
All stakeholders can verify the current state and history of products in real time. Hash-linked, signed revisions fix the order of supply chain events as they occur; blockchain timestamping ships with the full aqua-rs-sdk, scheduled for a later publication.

## Key benefits

### Detect counterfeits
Counterfeit products cost industries billions annually. A counterfeiter can produce a self-consistent Aqua tree of their own; what they cannot do is forge the legitimate manufacturer's signatures, because forging another party's signature is computationally infeasible. Verifiers who check that records are signed by the expected manufacturer keys can therefore identify fakes at any step of the supply chain.

### Improve recall efficiency
When safety issues arise, rapid recall is critical. Aqua Protocol enables precise identification of affected products, reducing recall scope and cost. Complete provenance data shows exactly which batches are impacted and where they were distributed.

### Increase consumer trust
Consumers increasingly demand transparency about product origins and manufacturing. Scannable QR codes linked to Aqua trees let customers verify authenticity, ethical sourcing, and quality standards, building brand trust.

### Streamline compliance
Regulatory compliance requires extensive documentation and audit trails. Aqua Protocol automatically creates tamper-evident records that support regulatory audits, reducing compliance costs and audit preparation time.

### Enable ethical sourcing
Prove that products meet ethical standards for labor practices, environmental impact, and sustainable sourcing. Signed, tamper-evident records counter "ethics washing" with verifiable documentation of responsible practices.

### Reduce liability
Complete documentation of supply chain events reduces liability exposure. When disputes arise, the signed record shows what was recorded, when, and by whom.

## How it works

### 1. Product registration
When a product enters the supply chain:
- Genesis revision is created with product identifier
- Initial metadata recorded (SKU, batch number, origin)
- Product data is validated against a product template
- Manufacturer signs the genesis revision
- Product receives its own Aqua tree

### 2. Supply chain events
As the product moves through the supply chain:
- Each event creates a new revision (transfer, inspection, processing)
- Event data includes timestamp, location, responsible party
- Each party signs their contributions to the tree
- Related records are connected with anchor revisions and links
- The tree grows to include the complete product history

### 3. Multi-party signatures
When custody transfers between parties:
- Sending party signs transfer revision
- Receiving party verifies and countersigns
- Both signatures reference the same state
- The tree preserves the order of both signatures
- Reduces disputes about transfer content or order

### 4. Verification
At any point, stakeholders can verify:
- Scan product QR code to retrieve the Aqua tree
- Verify all cryptographic signatures
- Check revision ordering and template conformance
- Validate the integrity of the entire tree
- Confirm product authenticity and history

## Real-world scenarios

### Scenario 1: pharmaceutical supply chain

**Challenge**: A pharmaceutical company must track medication from manufacturing through distribution to pharmacies while maintaining cold chain compliance and detecting counterfeits.

**Solution**:
- Each medication batch gets genesis revision when manufactured
- Quality control inspections add signed revisions
- Temperature monitors record readings as revisions
- Each distributor transfer is signed by both parties
- Pharmacies verify medication authenticity by scanning
- Regulators can audit the complete history from manufacturer to patient

**Benefits**: Counterfeit detection, cold chain compliance, regulatory audit trails, patient safety, streamlined recalls

### Scenario 2: organic food certification

**Challenge**: Consumers want to verify that "organic" products are genuinely organic from farm to store, but current systems rely on paper certificates that can be forged.

**Solution**:
- Farms create genesis revision with organic certification
- Certification body signs the certification revision
- Each processing step is documented and signed
- Distributors add transport and storage revisions
- Retailers sign final product placement
- Consumers scan QR code to verify organic provenance

**Benefits**: Verified organic claims, consumer confidence, brand differentiation, premium pricing justification

### Scenario 3: automotive parts traceability

**Challenge**: An automotive manufacturer needs to track critical safety components through multiple supplier tiers to ensure authenticity and enable recalls if defects are discovered.

**Solution**:
- Tier 3 supplier creates genesis for raw materials
- Tier 2 supplier adds processing revisions with quality checks
- Tier 1 supplier integrates components into assemblies with anchor revisions and links
- OEM manufacturer links assemblies into final vehicles
- Each VIN is linked to component Aqua trees
- Defective component traced to exact source and all affected vehicles

**Benefits**: Counterfeit detection, rapid recall execution, root cause analysis, supplier accountability, quality improvement

### Scenario 4: coffee supply chain transparency

**Challenge**: Coffee roasters want to prove fair trade practices and sustainable sourcing to increasingly conscious consumers, but existing certification systems are opaque.

**Solution**:
- Coffee farmers create genesis with harvest data
- Fair trade auditor signs certification revision
- Exporters document transport and quality grading
- Importers add customs and warehousing data
- Roasters link to final packaged products
- Consumers scan bags to see complete farm-to-cup journey

**Benefits**: Verified ethical sourcing, consumer transparency, premium pricing, farmer accountability, brand storytelling

## Best practices

### Genesis creation
- Create genesis as early as possible in supply chain
- Include comprehensive product metadata
- Use templates for standardized, validated data
- Sign genesis immediately to establish authenticity
- Have auditors or regulators countersign genesis for high-value or regulated products

### Event granularity
- Balance detail with practical overhead
- Record all custody transfers
- Document critical quality checkpoints
- Capture regulatory compliance events
- Skip routine events that don't add value

### Multi-party coordination
- Establish clear protocols for handoffs
- Both parties must sign custody transfers
- Verify previous signatures before adding new ones
- Communicate tree updates to all stakeholders
- Maintain consistent timestamp standards

### Data privacy
- Aqua trees store hashes, not sensitive content
- Use the tree method for [selective disclosure](/docs/v4.0.0/schema-reference/selective-disclosure)
- Share trees only with authorized parties
- Consider what metadata is included
- Redact sensitive fields before sharing with external parties

### Verification strategy
- Verify products at key checkpoints
- Check the complete tree for high-value items
- Spot-check for routine items
- Use the strict verification policy for critical events
- Train staff on verification procedures

### Batch operations
- Aggregate related products under a shared anchor revision
- Group similar products into one verifiable batch structure
- Balance aggregation granularity with per-item traceability
- Implement automated batching in integration systems
- Monitor batch sizes for optimization

## Technical advantages

### Scalability
- Object revisions are lightweight, one Aqua tree per product
- Anchor revisions with links enable hierarchical organization
- Batch anchors aggregate many items into one verifiable structure
- Offline verification after tree retrieval
- Distributed storage prevents bottlenecks

### Interoperability
- JSON-based format for easy integration
- Multiple SDK options (Rust for v4, JavaScript for v3)
- Standard cryptographic primitives
- Platform-agnostic verification
- Compatible with existing supply chain systems

### Flexibility
- Template system for industry-specific schemas
- Custom, template-defined revision kinds for unique requirements
- Multiple signature methods for different parties
- Hash algorithm choice (SHA3-256 default, BLAKE3-256 optional)
- Extensible for future needs

### Security
- SHA3-256 hashing makes collision attacks computationally infeasible
- Ed25519 and ECDSA signatures provide strong authentication
- Hash-linked revisions make history tampering detectable
- Merkle trees enable selective disclosure
- Cryptographic proof instead of trust

## Common questions

**Q: How do we handle products without digital interfaces?**
A: Use QR codes, NFC tags, or printed Aqua tree identifiers on packaging. The Aqua tree exists independently of the physical product. Low-cost printed codes work for most products.

**Q: What if a supply chain participant doesn't want to use Aqua Protocol?**
A: The record can continue without them, but their contribution won't carry cryptographic proof, and the gap is visible to anyone verifying the tree.

**Q: How do we handle returns and reverse logistics?**
A: Add revision documenting the return event, including reason and condition. The tree naturally grows to include forward and reverse movements, maintaining complete product history.

**Q: Can we integrate with existing traceability systems?**
A: Yes. Aqua Protocol can wrap existing systems, adding cryptographic proof to current tracking data. Legacy system data can be hashed and stored in revisions.

**Q: What about supply chain privacy?**
A: Aqua trees contain only hashes by default, not business-sensitive data. Share trees selectively with authorized parties. Use the tree method for selective disclosure of specific fields to certain stakeholders.

**Q: How do we handle lost or damaged products?**
A: Add revision documenting loss or damage event with relevant details. The Aqua tree preserves the history up to that point, supporting insurance claims and investigations.

**Q: Does verification require a blockchain or network connection?**
A: No. Verification of trees works offline using cryptographic signatures and hashes. Blockchain and TSA timestamping is defined by the full protocol and ships with the full SDK, scheduled for a later publication.
